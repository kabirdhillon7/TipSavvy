Get the PNGs

  1. Capture the app on each simulator. iPhone covers both iPhone sizes.

    Scripts/capture-app-store-screenshots.sh iphone
Scripts/capture-app-store-screenshots.sh ipad

  2. For Duo, fold or unfold the simulator in Xcode's Device Hub first, then:

    Scripts/capture-app-store-screenshots.sh duo-outer "TipSavvy Shots Duo"
Scripts/capture-app-store-screenshots.sh duo-inner "TipSavvy Shots Duo"

  3. Render every size that has captures:

    Scripts/render-app-store-screenshots.py

  4. Upload from AppStoreScreenshots/final/<device>/. Files are flattened RGB PNGs at Apple's exact sizes.
