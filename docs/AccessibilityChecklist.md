# TipSavvy Accessibility Verification Checklist

Use this checklist before claiming App Store accessibility support. The claim is that TipSavvy is VoiceOver-friendly, Larger Text-friendly, Dark Interface-ready, Reduced Motion-aware, Differentiates Without Color Alone, and supports Sufficient Contrast after manual review.

## Common Tasks To Verify

- Calculate a tip from a bill amount.
- Change tip percentage and split count.
- Choose a rounding mode.
- Save a named calculation.
- Open, copy/share, rename, and delete a saved tip.
- Change settings, view privacy information, and use support actions.

## Feature Checks

- VoiceOver: complete the common tasks using announced labels, values, hints, and grouped summaries.
- Larger Text: verify calculator, saved tips, saved detail, and settings remain readable at large accessibility text sizes.
- Dark Interface: verify all main screens remain usable in Dark Mode.
- Differentiate Without Color Alone: verify selected states, destructive actions, and errors use text/icons/labels in addition to color.
- Reduced Motion: verify animated transitions do not block task completion with Reduce Motion enabled.
- Sufficient Contrast: verify text, controls, and glass panels remain readable in normal and increased contrast modes.

## Do Not Claim

- Captions: TipSavvy has no video/audio content requiring captions.
- Audio Descriptions: TipSavvy has no video content requiring audio descriptions.
- Full Voice Control support: only claim after completing a dedicated Voice Control pass.
